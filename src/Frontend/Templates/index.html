Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, doctor credentials, and consultations.

How does the Digital Hospital OPD Pass work?

Once your booking is confirmed, an official Digital Hospital Pass is generated with your unique Appointment ID, assigned OPD Token number, and QR code. You can print or show it on your mobile device at the hospital reception for fast-track entry.

Are the doctors verified by the Medical Council?

Yes! 100% of clinicians on DoctorP are verified against their National Medical Commission (NMC) or State Medical Council registration numbers before their profiles and calendar availability are made public.

How do Telehealth Online Video Consultations work?

If you book a Video Consultation, simply click "Join Call" in your patient dashboard 5 minutes prior to your slot. The consultation runs in high-definition encrypted WebRTC video directly inside your browser without needing any third-party app.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can cancel an active appointment anytime directly from your dashboard. The reserved slot is automatically released in real time back to the public pool so another patient in need can book it.
